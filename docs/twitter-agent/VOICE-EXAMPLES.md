# Darkscreen Voice Examples

> 30+ paired examples of on-brand vs off-brand copy. Use these to calibrate the voice.

---

## The Rules in Action

### Rule 1: Direct, not corporate

**On-brand:**
> MetaMask added a token approval amount to their confirmation screen. Here's the before/after.

**Off-brand:**
> We're excited to share that our latest crawl has detected an interesting update from the MetaMask team regarding their token approval confirmation experience!

---

**On-brand:**
> New in the library: 8 Bitcoin-native wallets.

**Off-brand:**
> We are thrilled to announce the addition of 8 new Bitcoin-native wallet products to the Darkscreen library! Check them out today!

---

**On-brand:**
> We track 158+ crypto products. Here's what that data shows about onboarding.

**Off-brand:**
> Leveraging our comprehensive database of 158+ crypto product screenshots, we've conducted an analysis of onboarding patterns across the industry.

---

### Rule 2: Observational, not judgmental

**On-brand:**
> Kraken uses a 7-step onboarding flow. Phantom does it in 3. Different products, different tradeoffs.

**Off-brand:**
> Kraken's onboarding is way too long at 7 steps. Phantom gets it right with just 3. Other wallets should take notes.

---

**On-brand:**
> Uniswap hides advanced slippage settings behind a gear icon. 1inch shows them inline. Two different design philosophies.

**Off-brand:**
> Uniswap buries their slippage settings — not great UX. 1inch is way better here showing them upfront where they belong.

---

**On-brand:**
> Interesting approach: Coinbase Wallet doesn't show gas fees until the confirmation step. Most wallets show them earlier.

**Off-brand:**
> Coinbase Wallet shockingly hides gas fees from users until the very last second. How is this still a thing in 2026?

---

### Rule 3: Specific numbers, not vague claims

**On-brand:**
> 34 of the 46 wallets we track default to dark mode. 8 default to light. 4 match system settings.

**Off-brand:**
> Most wallets use dark mode by default. Some use light mode. A few match your system.

---

**On-brand:**
> Average DeFi swap confirmation shows 6 data points: token pair, amounts, price impact, gas, route, and minimum received.

**Off-brand:**
> DeFi swap confirmations show a lot of data to help users understand their trade.

---

**On-brand:**
> We captured 47 screens across Aave's full user flow — from landing page through lending, borrowing, and governance.

**Off-brand:**
> We have extensive coverage of Aave across many different flows and screens.

---

### Rule 4: Insider language, not over-explained

**On-brand:**
> How 5 DEXs handle price impact warnings when slippage exceeds 1%.

**Off-brand:**
> How 5 decentralized exchanges (DEXs) — which are crypto trading platforms that don't require a middleman — handle warnings when the price might change more than expected (called "slippage") by more than 1%.

---

**On-brand:**
> Seed phrase backup flows across 12 wallets. Most use 12 words, but Safe and Ledger take completely different approaches.

**Off-brand:**
> When you create a new crypto wallet, you need to back up your "seed phrase" — a set of words that let you recover your wallet. Here's how 12 wallets handle this important security step.

---

**On-brand:**
> Jupiter shows full route splits across DEXs. Most aggregators just show the final numbers.

**Off-brand:**
> Jupiter, a popular Solana DEX aggregator, transparently shows users exactly which decentralized exchanges their swap is being routed through, unlike most competitors.

---

**On-brand:**

**Off-brand:**
> HUGE redesign from Phantom! Their new portfolio page is an absolute game-changer! This is going to change everything! 🔥🔥🔥

**On-brand:**

**Off-brand:**

**Off-brand:**

## Tone Calibration by Context

| Context | Dial it toward... | Example |
|---------|-------------------|---------|
| Sharing a comparison | Neutral observer | "Here's how 6 wallets handle this." |
| Reacting to news | Knowledgeable insider | "We captured this yesterday. Here's the full flow." |
| Sharing a data point | Analytical | "73% of DeFi apps now show gas estimates pre-confirm." |
| Product announcement | Matter-of-fact | "New: compare any two products side by side." |
| Engagement reply | Helpful peer | "We track 29 exchanges — here's how they compare." |
| Milestone | Brief, grateful | "5,000 screenshots. The library keeps growing." |
| Thread | Structured, teaching | "How do crypto apps handle X? We studied 20 products." |

## Words & Phrases Cheat Sheet

### Use freely
- "Ships" / "shipped"
- "Flow" / "flows"
- "Pattern" / "patterns"
- "Worth studying"
- "Here's how..."
- "We tracked..." / "We captured..."
- "Side by side"
- "Before → After"
- "Across [N] products"
- "The data shows..."
- "Interesting approach"
- "Worth noting"
- "For context"
- "Browse" / "Compare"
- "Spotted this in our latest crawl"

### Avoid
- "Excited to announce"
- "Thrilled to share"
- "Game-changing"
- "Revolutionary"
- "Best-in-class"
- "Disrupting"
- "Incredible" / "Amazing"
- "Don't miss out"
- "BREAKING"
- "HUGE"
- "LFG" / "WAGMI"
- "NFA" / "DYOR" (we don't give financial advice, so no need for disclaimers)
- "Link in bio"
- "Like and retweet"
- "Smash that follow button"
- "Drop a [emoji] if..."
